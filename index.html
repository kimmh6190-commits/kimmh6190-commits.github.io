Pulli Uploader - Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. About

Pulli Uploader is a personal desktop tool operated by its owner. It uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's own accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Terms of Service

  • The tool is used only by its owner for the owner's own content.
  • Videos are uploaded only after the owner reviews and approves the title, description and privacy setting.
  • The owner is responsible for the content posted and for following each platform's rules.
  • The tool is provided as is, without warranty.

3. Privacy Policy

  • Information accessed: when the owner authorizes the tool, it receives an access token for the owner's own account so that it can upload videos. It also reads the owner's account display name to show which account is selected.
  • Information collected from other people: none. The tool does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data about any other person.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sent to any server other than the platform that issued them.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with third parties.
  • Deletion: the owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in the settings of each platform, and can delete the stored tokens from the computer.
